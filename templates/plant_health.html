Recording and AI workflow

The full video is saved as MP4. After stopping, MushroomGuard extracts review frames at the configured interval (default 5 seconds). Label each recording with the stage you personally observed to build a crop-specific YOLO dataset. No trained weights are bundled; predictions remain unavailable until a model is trained and approved. A prediction never changes the growing-unit stage without your confirmation.

AI model status

Video analysis currently uses a crop-specific YOLO image classifier on sampled frames from the saved MP4. It averages frame probabilities; it is not a temporal video/action model. The v1.5 package includes Methi image candidates and training/inference code, but no trained model. Stage 3 data and independent tray groups are still required before four-stage training. Other microgreens and mushroom varieties need their own labelled datasets and models.
